How this tracker works

The views

Dashboard
Read-only. What is overdue, what lands in the next seven days ranked by date then priority, per-course progress, and a fourteen-day workload forecast.
CS440 · CS391 · CS360 · ENG216
One view per class, colour-coded. This is where you actually add, edit and complete work.
Personal
Anything that is not coursework. Five fields, deliberately simple. Kept out of every Dashboard and Analytics number.
Grades
Weights and scores per course, your current grade, and what you would need to average on everything left.
Analytics
Cross-course roll-up: outlook, priority, status and type of work.
Groups & Schedules
Reference only. Your CS440 project groups and both tentative class schedules.

Fields you fill in

Task
Short name. A task with no name is not counted anywhere.
Details / What's Required
The full description: what the assignment asks for, what to hand in.
Type
Reading, Problem Set, Lab, Exam / Quiz and so on. Exam, Paper and Project also count as milestones for “Next big one”.
Priority & Status
Dropdowns. Status flipping to Done stamps the completion date automatically, and clears it if you flip back.
Due Date
Everything time-based keys off this.
Est. Min
Rough minutes. Drives the workload hours and the Start By date.

Columns that calculate themselves

Days Left
Due date minus today. Negative means overdue. Blank once the task is done.
Start By
Working backward from the due date at about 90 focused minutes a day. It turns amber once that day has arrived and the task still isn't done.
Surfaces on
The day a task stops being “Later” and joins today's list — whichever comes first, 21 days before it's due or its Start By date.

Active vs Later

A project due in December isn't something you can act on in August. Anything further out than the horizon waits in Later: still editable, still counted in Analytics and in your completion percentage, just not in today's list. Add notes and refine estimates freely — it stays quiet.

It surfaces on its own
When the surface date arrives, or the moment it goes overdue. Overdue work always surfaces, no exceptions.
Starting it surfaces it
Set the status to In Progress or Waiting / Blocked and it joins the active list straight away.
Or do it by hand
Surface on the row, or the Timing box in the edit dialog, which also lets you push a task back to a date you pick. Clear that date to go back to automatic.
Nothing is hidden
The dashboard's On the horizon card lists what's waiting and when each item arrives. Every course tab has a Later filter. Settings changes the 21 days.

What the colours mean

Red row
Overdue and not done.
Amber row
Due within three days.
Faded, struck through
Done. Left in place so your completion stats stay honest.

Course colours identify the class and never carry urgency; the pills and row stripes carry urgency and never identify a class.

Good habits

Put an estimate on everything
Est. Min is what makes Start By, the workload chart and the hours numbers work at all.
Fill in Grades at the start of term
Ten minutes with four syllabi, and the “what do I need on the final” answer is there all semester.
Export now and then
Data lives in this browser only. Settings → Export JSON is your backup.
